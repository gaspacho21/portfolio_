# Fredericksburg Civil Rights StoryMap
**Role:** Lead Designer & Developer | **Recognition:** U.S. Civil Rights Trail

This project involved the end-to-end lifecycle of designing and developing a digital narrative for the Fredericksburg Civil Rights Trail. It was officially inducted into the U.S. Civil Rights Trail, serving as a permanent historical resource for the city.
